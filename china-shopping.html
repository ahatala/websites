Shopping at Ruoholahti

Start with K-Citymarket Helsinki Ruoholahti and select it on K-Ruoka. Ingredient searches and the specialist product examples across the site use the K-Ruoka catalogue checked on 11 September 2026. Live Ruoholahti availability has not been verified; check the selected store and current package labels. No peanut ingredients; “may contain peanuts” is okay for this household.

Buy fresh meat, seafood and delicate produce near their cooking days, or freeze according to the pack. Check use-by dates rather than assuming everything will keep for a week.
