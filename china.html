A little head start

  • Friday’s dumpling dough is made from plain flour. Fold the mushroom batch first and give it a different shape.
  • Saturday’s mango puddings need three hours to chill. Sunday’s sesame biscuits replace sesame balls.
  • For hot pot, use a shared vegetable pot and a separate beef pot. A two-pot setup on the hob works too.

Make it a festival

Give everyone a dumpling-folding station on Friday. On Sunday, arrange the hot-pot vegetables into a colourful serving platter.
