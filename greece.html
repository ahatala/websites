A little head start

  • Check the filo thawing instructions before Tuesday. Save enough for Sunday’s feta parcels, following pack storage directions.
  • Thursday’s giouvetsi needs a long, gentle cook. Start early or prepare the beef ahead.
  • Sunday: make the two moussaka fillings side by side. Tiropita is a light lunch; doughnuts finish the day.
