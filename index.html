3 weeks · 3 countries · one family festival

Family International Food Festival

A three-week dinner adventure through Greece, China and the United Kingdom, with weekend breakfasts, lunches and desserts. Designed around ingredients readily available in Helsinki.

Make it a real family festival

Each weekend, add one small cultural activity: learn a few words from the country, find it on a map, play local music, or let everyone rate the dishes. Keep the judging playful and announce a winning country at the end.
