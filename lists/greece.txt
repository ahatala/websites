NOTES
Suggested pack sizes are planning aids, not confirmed Ruoholahti stock. For drained tins check the label.
Stock is measured prepared, meat is raw, rice/pasta/noodles are dry. Frying oil is included as a full batch.
Shop fresh items near the day of use, or freeze according to the pack; use-by dates determine storage.
Product labels and availability can change. Check the current labels for peanut ingredients.
