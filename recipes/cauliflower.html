Sunday / Dinner

The cauliflower Sunday roast

Cauliflower cheese for three, roast potatoes, Yorkshires, vegetables and onion gravy.

Serves 3Meat-free for all 380 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 3 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Cauliflower (trimmed)800 g
  • Potatoes750 g
  • Carrots300 g
  • Frozen peas200 g
  • Cheddar180 g
  • Milk550 ml
  • Plain flour145 g
  • Butter60 g
  • Eggs2
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)55 ml
  • Onions200 g
  • Vegetable stock (from cubes or concentrate)400 ml prepared
  • Soy sauce10 ml
  • Dijon mustard10 g
  • Dried thyme½ tsp
  • Salt¾ tsp
  • Black pepper½ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Whisk 75 g flour, eggs, 150 ml milk and a quarter teaspoon salt into a batter; rest. Peel and cut potatoes, boil for 8 minutes, drain and roughen. Toss in 30 ml oil and roast for 50–60 minutes, turning halfway.
  2. Cut cauliflower into florets and steam for 6–8 minutes until just tender; drain very well. Melt 40 g butter, stir in 40 g flour and cook for 1 minute. Whisk in the remaining 400 ml milk gradually. Simmer until thick, then stir in mustard, 140 g cheddar and half the pepper.
  3. Put cauliflower in a roughly 20 cm baking dish, pour over sauce and top with the remaining cheese. Bake for 25 minutes until golden. Remove, cover loosely and keep warm while the Yorkshires bake. Potatoes may stay in the oven to finish crisping.
  4. For gravy, soften sliced onions in the remaining 20 g butter for 15 minutes. Stir in the remaining 30 g flour, then stock, soy, thyme and remaining pepper and salt. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Raise oven to 210°C fan. Divide the remaining 25 ml oil among six metal muffin tin wells and heat for 5 minutes. Pour batter evenly into the hot wells and bake for 20–25 minutes without opening the door until risen and golden.
  6. Steam sliced carrots for 8–10 minutes and cook peas according to their packet. Serve everything together, with two small Yorkshires each.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
