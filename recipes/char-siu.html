Char siu-style pork & tofu

Sticky oven-roasted pork and a separate tofu portion, with pak choi and rice.

Serves 43 pork + 1 tofu65 min + 30 min marinating
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Pork shoulder, boneless600 g
  • Firm tofu200 g
  • Hoisin sauce (no peanut ingredients)60 g
  • Soy sauce30 ml
  • Honey30 g
  • Ground cinnamon¼ tsp
  • Black pepper¼ tsp
  • Garlic cloves3
  • Fresh ginger15 g
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)20 ml
  • Jasmine rice (dry)300 g
  • Pak choi500 g

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Cut pork into long strips about 3 cm thick and tofu into thick slabs. Mix hoisin, soy, honey, cinnamon, black pepper, grated ginger and two crushed garlic cloves. Reserve one quarter in a clean bowl for tofu. Marinate pork and tofu separately in their sauce for at least 30 minutes in the fridge.
  2. Heat the oven to 190°C fan. Put pork and its marinade in a lined roasting dish; put tofu and its marinade in another small dish. Roast pork for 35–45 minutes, turning halfway, until the centre reaches 75°C for 30 seconds. Roast tofu for 25–30 minutes. Add a splash of water if the glaze begins to catch.
  3. Cook rice according to the packet. Slice pak choi, heat oil in a pan and fry the remaining chopped garlic for 30 seconds. Add greens and 40 ml water; cover for 2 minutes, then uncover and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Rest pork for 5 minutes, slice and serve with rice and greens; serve tofu separately. Do not use uncooked marinade as a finishing sauce.
Kitchen note

The hoisin product must have no peanut ingredients; “may contain” is acceptable for this household.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
