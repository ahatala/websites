Chicken & vegetable chow mein

A colourful noodle dinner with an easy tofu portion.

Serves 43 chicken + 1 tofu30 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Boneless chicken450 g
  • Firm tofu150 g
  • Wheat noodles (dry)300 g
  • Carrots200 g
  • Chinese cabbage300 g
  • Bell peppers200 g
  • Spring onions50 g
  • Garlic cloves3
  • Fresh ginger15 g
  • Soy sauce45 ml
  • Hoisin sauce (no peanut ingredients)30 g
  • Toasted sesame oil10 ml
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)30 ml

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Cook noodles according to their packet, drain and toss with sesame oil. Thinly slice chicken, cube tofu and cut vegetables into thin strips. Mix soy sauce, hoisin and 50 ml water in a bowl.
  2. Stir-fry chicken in 10 ml oil for 7–10 minutes, until cooked through at 75°C for 30 seconds. Set aside. In a separate small pan, brown tofu in 5 ml oil for 6–8 minutes.
  3. In a clean large wok or pan, heat the remaining 15 ml oil. Fry chopped garlic and ginger for 30 seconds, add carrots, cabbage and pepper, and stir-fry for 5 minutes.
  4. Add noodles, sauce and spring onions; toss until hot. Move one quarter of the noodle mixture to the tofu pan. Add chicken to the larger portion. Heat both through and serve.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
