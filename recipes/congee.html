Saturday / Breakfast

Breakfast congee

A gentle rice porridge with eggs, spring onion and crisp onion topping.

Serves 4Meat-free for everyone65 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Jasmine rice (dry)180 g
  • Eggs4
  • Fresh ginger15 g
  • Spring onions50 g
  • Onions100 g
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)20 ml
  • Soy sauce20 ml
  • Toasted sesame oil5 ml
  • Salt¼ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Rinse rice, then put in a large saucepan with 1.5 litres water, sliced ginger and salt. Bring to a boil, reduce heat and simmer partly covered for 50–60 minutes, stirring occasionally, until creamy. Add hot water if too thick.
  2. Boil eggs for 9–10 minutes, cool under cold water, peel and halve. Thinly slice onion and fry in rapeseed oil for 10–15 minutes until golden; drain on kitchen paper.
  3. Remove ginger slices from the porridge. Divide between four bowls and top with eggs, crisp onions and chopped spring onions.
  4. Mix soy and sesame oil and let everyone add a little to their bowl.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
