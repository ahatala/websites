Lentil & mushroom cottage pie

A deeply savoury filling under a fork-ridged potato roof.

Serves 4Meat-free for everyone65 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Tinned green or brown lentils480 g drained
  • Mushrooms400 g
  • Onions200 g
  • Carrots250 g
  • Garlic cloves2
  • Tomato purée30 g
  • Vegetable stock (from cubes or concentrate)400 ml prepared
  • Soy sauce15 ml
  • Dried thyme1 tsp
  • Plain flour20 g
  • Olive oil25 ml
  • Potatoes1000 g
  • Milk150 ml
  • Butter40 g
  • Cheddar60 g
  • Salt¾ tsp
  • Black pepper½ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Heat the oven to 190°C fan. Peel and chop potatoes. Boil for 15–20 minutes until tender; drain and mash with milk, butter and half the salt.
  2. Dice onions and carrots and soften in olive oil for 8 minutes. Add chopped mushrooms and cook for 8 minutes until their liquid evaporates. Add garlic, thyme and tomato purée, then flour; stir for 1 minute.
  3. Stir in stock, drained lentils, soy sauce, pepper and remaining salt. Simmer for 10 minutes until thick. Spoon into a roughly 20 × 25 cm baking dish.
  4. Spread mash over the filling, roughen with a fork and scatter with grated cheddar. Bake for 25 minutes until bubbling and golden.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
