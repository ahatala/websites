Sunday / Dessert

Apple crumble & custard

Tart apples under a buttery, golden crumble.

Serves 3Meat-free for all 345 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 3 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Apples500 g
  • Plain flour90 g
  • Butter50 g
  • Caster sugar55 g
  • Ground cinnamon½ tsp
  • Ready-made custard300 ml

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C fan. Core and slice apples. Put in a small baking dish with 20 g sugar, cinnamon and 30 ml water.
  2. Rub cold cubed butter into flour until crumbly. Stir in the remaining 35 g sugar and scatter over the apples without pressing down.
  3. Bake for 30–35 minutes until the fruit is soft and the topping golden. Rest for 5 minutes.
  4. Warm custard according to the pack and pour over three portions of crumble.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
