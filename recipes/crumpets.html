Sunday / Breakfast

Homemade crumpets

Small bubbly griddle cakes, butter and jam. No specialist bakery trip.

Serves 3Makes 6 · two each35 min + 1 hr rising
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 3 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Plain flour180 g
  • Milk150 ml
  • Instant dried yeast1 tsp
  • Caster sugar5 g
  • Salt¼ tsp
  • Baking powder½ tsp
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)10 ml
  • Butter30 g
  • Jam90 g

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Whisk flour, yeast, sugar and salt with the lukewarm milk and 150 ml lukewarm water. Cover for 1 hour until bubbly.
  2. Stir baking powder into 15 ml water and beat into the batter. It should be thick but pourable; add a little more water if needed.
  3. Lightly oil a non-stick frying pan and metal crumpet or egg rings. Heat on medium-low. Put rings in the pan and fill each about 1 cm deep. Cook for 8–10 minutes until holes appear and the top is almost set.
  4. Remove rings carefully, turn and cook for 1–2 minutes. Repeat for six crumpets. Without rings, use smaller free-form rounds; they will be flatter and cook faster. Serve with butter and jam.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
