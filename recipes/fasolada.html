Fasolada

White beans, tomato and vegetables, with bread and feta.

Serves 4Meat-free for everyone45 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Tinned white beans720 g drained
  • Tinned chopped tomatoes400 g
  • Onions150 g
  • Carrots250 g
  • Celery150 g
  • Garlic cloves2
  • Olive oil30 ml
  • Vegetable stock (from cubes or concentrate)800 ml prepared
  • Dried oregano1 tsp
  • Bay leaves1
  • Crusty / sliced bread400 g
  • Feta150 g
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Black pepper½ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Dice the onion, carrots and celery. Soften in the olive oil in a large saucepan for 10 minutes. Add chopped garlic and stir for 1 minute.
  2. Add tomatoes, drained beans, prepared vegetable stock, oregano and bay leaf. Bring to a simmer and cook gently for 25 minutes until the carrots are tender.
  3. Mash a ladleful of beans against the side of the pan to thicken the soup. Remove the bay leaf. Season with salt and pepper, reducing the salt if the stock is already salty.
  4. Warm the bread and serve with bowls of soup and crumbled feta on the side.
Kitchen note

720 g drained beans is about three 400 g tins; check the drained weights.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
