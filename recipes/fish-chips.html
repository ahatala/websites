Thursday / Dinner

Fish & chips

Crisp battered white fish, oven chips, quick mushy peas and tartare sauce.

Serves 4Shared seafood meal · cod or haddock55 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Boneless cod or haddock fillets700 g
  • Potatoes1000 g
  • Plain flour180 g
  • Cornflour40 g
  • Baking powder1 tsp
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)1000 ml
  • Frozen peas400 g
  • Butter20 g
  • Lemons1
  • Mayonnaise100 g
  • Capers20 g
  • Gherkins40 g
  • Malt vinegar30 ml
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Black pepper½ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Heat the oven to 210°C fan. Cut potatoes into thick chips, boil for 5 minutes, drain and steam-dry. Toss with 30 ml of the oil and half the salt; roast for 35–40 minutes, turning halfway.
  2. Mix mayonnaise with chopped capers, chopped gherkins and half the lemon juice. Cook peas according to their packet, drain, then mash with butter, pepper and 30 ml hot water.
  3. Pat the fish dry and check for bones. Dust with 30 g of the flour. Mix the remaining 150 g flour, cornflour, baking powder and remaining salt. Whisk in 230 ml very cold water just before frying.
  4. Heat the remaining oil in a deep, heavy pan to 175°C, never filling more than one third. Dip fish into batter and carefully lower into oil, cooking in batches for 6–9 minutes depending on thickness, until crisp and cooked through. Check the thickest part reaches 75°C for 30 seconds.
  5. Drain on kitchen paper. Serve immediately with chips, peas, tartare, malt vinegar and the remaining lemon in wedges.
Kitchen note

Use a thermometer for the oil. The list includes a fresh batch of frying oil. For an oven-only version, buy breaded cod/haddock with no peanut ingredients, replace the fish-and-batter ingredients accordingly, and follow its packet.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
