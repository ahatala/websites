Saturday / Breakfast

A little Full English

A weekend breakfast for two meat-eaters and one meat-free diner.

Serves 32 meat + 1 vegetarian breakfast30 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 3 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Eggs3
  • Pork sausages200 g
  • Bacon100 g
  • Vegetarian sausages100 g
  • Baked beans400 g
  • Mushrooms250 g
  • Tomatoes300 g
  • Crusty / sliced bread180 g
  • Butter20 g
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)15 ml
  • Black pepper¼ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Heat the oven to 190°C fan. Put pork sausages and bacon on one tray, vegetarian sausages on a separate small tray. Cook according to the packs, allowing about 20–25 minutes for raw pork sausages; check they reach 75°C for 30 seconds.
  2. Halve tomatoes and slice mushrooms. Cook together in 10 ml oil in a separate pan for 8–10 minutes, seasoning with pepper. Warm baked beans in a saucepan.
  3. Fry eggs in the remaining 5 ml oil until whites and yolks are set to your preference. Toast and butter the bread.
  4. Build three plates: shared eggs, beans, mushrooms, tomatoes and toast, with pork and bacon on two plates and vegetarian sausages on one.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
