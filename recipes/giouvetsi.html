Beef giouvetsi

Slow-cooked beef and tomato orzo, just for the three attending.

Serves 33 beef portions · meat-free diner away2 hr 30 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 3 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Stewing beef600 g
  • Orzo (dry)240 g
  • Tinned chopped tomatoes400 g
  • Onions150 g
  • Garlic cloves2
  • Olive oil20 ml
  • Tomato purée20 g
  • Vegetable stock (from cubes or concentrate)700 ml prepared
  • Ground cinnamon½ tsp
  • Bay leaves1
  • Cheddar40 g
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Black pepper½ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Heat the oven to 160°C fan. Cut beef into 3 cm cubes. Brown in the oil in an ovenproof casserole in batches, then lift out. Soften chopped onion in the same pot for 8 minutes.
  2. Add chopped garlic and tomato purée; stir for 1 minute. Return the beef with tomatoes, 400 ml stock, cinnamon, bay, salt and pepper. Cover and bake for 1½–2 hours until fork-tender.
  3. Raise the oven to 180°C fan. Remove the bay leaf. Stir in the dry orzo and remaining 300 ml hot stock. Bake uncovered for 20–25 minutes, stirring halfway. Add a splash of hot water if the orzo is drying out before it is tender.
  4. Rest for 10 minutes; the orzo continues absorbing sauce. Serve with grated hard cheese.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
