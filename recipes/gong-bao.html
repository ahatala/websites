Saturday / Dinner

Gong bao-style chicken

A tangy, glossy stir-fry with peppers; no peanuts added.

Serves 43 chicken + 1 tofu35 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Boneless chicken450 g
  • Firm tofu150 g
  • Bell peppers300 g
  • Carrots200 g
  • Spring onions75 g
  • Fresh ginger20 g
  • Garlic cloves3
  • Soy sauce40 ml
  • Rice vinegar30 ml
  • Caster sugar20 g
  • Cornflour20 g
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)30 ml
  • Jasmine rice (dry)300 g
  • Chilli flakes (serve separately)2 g

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Cook rice according to the packet. Cube chicken and tofu. Mix soy, vinegar, sugar, cornflour and 120 ml water. Reserve one quarter of this clean sauce for the tofu pan.
  2. Brown tofu in 5 ml oil in a small pan for 6–8 minutes. In a larger pan, stir-fry chicken in 10 ml oil for 7–10 minutes, until it reaches 75°C for 30 seconds. Set each aside in separate bowls.
  3. Chop peppers and carrots into small pieces. Stir-fry in the remaining 15 ml oil for 5 minutes in a clean pan. Add chopped ginger, garlic and spring onion and fry for 1 minute.
  4. Divide vegetables 3:1 between the chicken and tofu pans. Stir each reserved sauce before adding. Simmer each for 2–3 minutes until glossy and hot throughout. Serve with rice and chilli flakes separately.
Kitchen note

A family adaptation of gong bao: the peanuts are omitted, with no nut replacement needed.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
