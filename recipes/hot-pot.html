Sunday / Dinner

The big family hot pot

A bubbling vegetable broth, a colourful spread and a little beef pot on the side.

Serves 4Shared meat-free pot + beef for 345 min + cooking at the table
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Vegetable stock (from cubes or concentrate)2500 ml prepared
  • Fresh ginger40 g
  • Garlic cloves4
  • Spring onions100 g
  • Mushrooms500 g
  • Pak choi400 g
  • Chinese cabbage400 g
  • Carrots200 g
  • Firm tofu600 g
  • Thinly sliced beef for hot pot450 g
  • Wheat noodles (dry)250 g
  • Soy sauce80 ml
  • Rice vinegar50 ml
  • Toasted sesame oil20 ml
  • Sesame seeds20 g
  • Chilli flakes (serve separately)2 g

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. You need two pots, separate ladles and tongs, and a safe tabletop cooker; alternatively keep both pots simmering on the hob and serve cooked batches at the table. Keep raw-meat plates and utensils separate from cooked food.
  2. Simmer stock with sliced ginger, two crushed garlic cloves and half the spring onions for 15 minutes. Transfer 750 ml broth to the separate meat pot; keep 1.75 litres in the shared pot. Bring both to a steady simmer.
  3. Slice mushrooms, wash and chop pak choi and cabbage, cut carrots very thinly and cube tofu. Arrange on platters. Keep the thinly sliced raw beef refrigerated until cooking. Cook noodles separately according to the packet and divide into bowls.
  4. Mix soy, vinegar and sesame oil with 50 ml water. Add the remaining finely chopped garlic and spring onions. Divide among dipping bowls and offer sesame seeds and chilli separately.
  5. Add small batches of mushrooms and carrots to the shared pot; simmer for 4–6 minutes, then add greens and tofu for 3–5 minutes, until hot and tender. Cook beef only in its separate pot in small batches until fully cooked; check a folded thick piece reaches 75°C for 30 seconds. Do not treat a brief dip as sufficient.
  6. Serve cooked food with noodles and dipping sauces, adding hot water to the pots as the broth reduces. Keep both pots simmering throughout service. Use clean eating utensils, not the raw-meat tongs.
Kitchen note

Amounts include beef for three and tofu to share with all four. This is a mild, supermarket-friendly hot pot; everyone can season their own dipping bowl.

More setup ideas: The Woks of Life’s hot-pot guide. Use our peanut-free ingredient list and separate-pot plan above.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
