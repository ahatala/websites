Friday / Dinner

Jiaozi dumpling night

Make, fold and share: pork-and-cabbage and mushroom dumplings.

Serves 424 pork + 8 mushroom dumplings90 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Plain flour380 g
  • Minced pork300 g
  • Mushrooms150 g
  • Chinese cabbage300 g
  • Spring onions50 g
  • Fresh ginger20 g
  • Garlic cloves2
  • Soy sauce60 ml
  • Rice vinegar40 ml
  • Toasted sesame oil15 ml
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)30 ml
  • Cucumber400 g
  • Salt¾ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Mix 360 g flour with half a teaspoon salt and 190 ml warm water, adding water a spoonful at a time if needed to form a firm dough. Knead for 8 minutes and cover to rest for 30 minutes. Reserve 20 g flour for rolling.
  2. Finely chop cabbage, mix with the remaining salt, rest 10 minutes and squeeze dry. Finely chop spring onion, ginger and one garlic clove. Divide these vegetables 3:1 between two bowls. Add pork, 15 ml soy and 5 ml sesame oil to the larger bowl.
  3. Finely chop mushrooms and fry in 5 ml rapeseed oil until dry; cool. Add to the smaller vegetable bowl with 5 ml soy and 5 ml sesame oil. Keep filling bowls and utensils separate.
  4. Divide dough into 32 pieces. Roll into thin 10–11 cm circles on the reserved flour. Fill 24 with pork mixture and 8 with mushroom mixture, dividing each filling evenly among its wrappers (about a heaped tablespoon each); pinch tightly shut. Use a different fold for the mushroom batch.
  5. Cook batches in separate non-stick pans. Use the remaining 25 ml rapeseed oil across the batches. Fry bases for 2 minutes, add water to 5 mm deep and cover. Steam for 8–10 minutes; check pork filling reaches 75°C for 30 seconds. Uncover and let water evaporate to crisp the bases.
  6. Mix 30 ml soy and 30 ml vinegar with 20 ml water as dipping sauce. Smash and chop cucumber; dress with the remaining 10 ml soy, 10 ml vinegar, 5 ml sesame oil and crushed garlic clove. Serve alongside.
Kitchen note

Homemade wrappers avoid relying on frozen dumpling skins. Fold the mushroom batch first. A wide frying pan with a well-fitting lid is sufficient.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
