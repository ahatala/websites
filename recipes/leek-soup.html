Leek & potato soup

A cosy bowl with crisp cheddar toasties for dunking.

Serves 4Meat-free for everyone40 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Leeks500 g
  • Potatoes500 g
  • Onions150 g
  • Butter50 g
  • Vegetable stock (from cubes or concentrate)1000 ml prepared
  • Milk200 ml
  • Crusty / sliced bread400 g
  • Cheddar200 g
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Black pepper½ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Wash leeks carefully and slice. Chop onion and soften both in 25 g butter for 10 minutes without browning.
  2. Add peeled, diced potatoes and stock. Simmer for 20 minutes until very tender. Blend until smooth, stir in milk, salt and pepper, and warm through gently.
  3. Make four sandwiches with bread and cheddar. Spread the remaining 25 g butter thinly on the outsides. Pan-toast over medium-low heat for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and melted inside.
  4. Cut the toasties into fingers or triangles and serve with the soup.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
