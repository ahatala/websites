Sunday / Dessert

Loukoumades

A small batch of warm honey-and-cinnamon doughnuts.

Serves 4Makes about 16 small doughnuts35 min + 1 hr rising
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Plain flour200 g
  • Instant dried yeast1 tsp
  • Caster sugar10 g
  • Salt¼ tsp
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)750 ml
  • Honey80 g
  • Ground cinnamon1 tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar and salt. Beat in 200 ml lukewarm water to make a thick, sticky batter. Cover and leave in a warm place for about 1 hour until bubbly and doubled.
  2. Warm honey with 20 ml water in a small pan; set aside. Heat rapeseed oil to 175°C in a deep heavy pan no more than one third full. Use a thermometer and keep children away from the frying area.
  3. Using two damp teaspoons, gently lower small spoonfuls of batter into the oil. Fry in batches for 3–4 minutes, turning, until golden and cooked inside. Keep the oil around 175°C and drain on kitchen paper.
  4. Toss with the honey syrup, sprinkle with cinnamon and serve warm.
Kitchen note

Frying oil is counted as a whole batch in the shopping list, not as the amount eaten. Use a narrower suitable pot if 750 ml does not give enough depth; never overfill.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
