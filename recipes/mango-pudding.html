Saturday / Dessert

Mango pudding

Sunny mango-and-coconut cups, set without gelatine.

Serves 4Meat-free for everyone20 min + 3 hr chilling
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Mango flesh (fresh or frozen)300 g
  • Coconut milk250 ml
  • Caster sugar30 g
  • Agar-agar powder2.5 g

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Blend thawed mango flesh until smooth and let it reach room temperature. You need about 300 ml purée.
  2. Whisk agar powder and sugar into coconut milk in a saucepan. Bring to a boil while stirring, then simmer for 2 minutes to dissolve the agar; follow the product’s activation instructions if they differ.
  3. Quickly whisk in the mango purée over low heat until smooth and steaming. Do not let it cool in the pan. Divide among four small glasses.
  4. Cool, then refrigerate for at least 3 hours until set. Serve chilled in the glasses.
Kitchen note

Use pure agar powder, not a sweetened gelling mixture. Strength varies by brand; follow the pack dosage for roughly 550 ml mixture if different. K-Ruoka lists Meira agar agar.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
