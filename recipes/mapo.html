Mild mapo-style tofu

Mushrooms and tofu in a savoury sauce, with rice and garlicky greens.

Serves 4Meat-free for everyone35 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Soft tofu600 g
  • Mushrooms250 g
  • Fresh ginger20 g
  • Garlic cloves3
  • Spring onions50 g
  • Vegan miso paste30 g
  • Soy sauce20 ml
  • Vegetable stock (from cubes or concentrate)300 ml prepared
  • Cornflour15 g
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)25 ml
  • Jasmine rice (dry)300 g
  • Pak choi400 g
  • Chilli flakes (serve separately)2 g
  • Black pepper¼ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Cook rice according to its packet. Cut tofu into 2 cm cubes. Finely chop mushrooms, ginger, garlic and spring onions.
  2. Heat 15 ml oil in a wide pan and fry mushrooms for 7 minutes until browned. Add ginger, two thirds of the garlic and the white spring onion parts; stir for 1 minute.
  3. Mix miso with the stock and soy sauce; pour into the pan. Add tofu and simmer gently for 6–8 minutes. Mix cornflour with 30 ml cold water, fold into the sauce and simmer for 2 minutes until lightly thickened. Add black pepper and green spring onion tops.
  4. In another pan, heat the remaining 10 ml oil with the remaining garlic. Add chopped pak choi and 30 ml water, cover for 2 minutes, then stir-fry uncovered for 2 minutes. Serve with rice; put chilli flakes on the table.
Kitchen note

This is a mild K-store adaptation using vegan miso instead of doubanjiang. It does not reproduce the heat or flavour of traditional Sichuan mapo tofu. Firm tofu also works if soft tofu is unavailable.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
