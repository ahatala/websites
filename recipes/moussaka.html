Sunday / Dinner

Moussaka, two ways

One beef bake and one small lentil-and-mushroom bake, with shared layers.

Serves 43 beef + 1 lentil & mushroom1 hr 50 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Aubergines800 g
  • Potatoes600 g
  • Minced beef450 g
  • Tinned green or brown lentils120 g drained
  • Mushrooms100 g
  • Tinned chopped tomatoes600 g
  • Onions200 g
  • Garlic cloves3
  • Tomato purée20 g
  • Olive oil60 ml
  • Ground cinnamon½ tsp
  • Dried oregano1 tsp
  • Butter50 g
  • Plain flour50 g
  • Milk600 ml
  • Cheddar60 g
  • Ground nutmeg¼ tsp
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Black pepper½ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Slice potatoes and aubergines into 5 mm rounds. Toss with 45 ml oil and half the salt. Roast on lined trays for 25–30 minutes, turning once, until tender.
  2. Soften chopped onion in the remaining 15 ml oil for 8 minutes. Add garlic and purée for 1 minute, then tomatoes, cinnamon, oregano, remaining salt and pepper. Simmer for 15 minutes. Divide this meat-free sauce: three quarters into one bowl, one quarter into another.
  3. Brown the beef in a large pan for 8–10 minutes, breaking it up; stir in the larger sauce portion and simmer for 10 minutes. In a separate small pan, dry-fry chopped mushrooms until their liquid evaporates, then add drained lentils and the smaller sauce portion; simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Melt butter in a saucepan, stir in flour and cook for 1 minute. Gradually whisk in milk. Simmer, whisking, until thick; stir in nutmeg and half the cheese.
  5. Reduce the oven to 180°C fan. Use a roughly 20 × 20 cm dish for the beef and a small 12 × 15 cm dish for the meat-free portion. Split potatoes, aubergines and béchamel 3:1 between them. Layer potatoes, aubergine, filling and béchamel; finish with remaining cheese.
  6. Bake for 35–40 minutes until bubbling, golden and hot throughout (beef filling 75°C for 30 seconds). Rest for 15 minutes before serving.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
