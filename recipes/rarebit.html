Saturday / Dinner

Welsh rarebit & tomato soup

Mustardy cheese toast, a little soup and a green salad.

Serves 3Meat-free for all 330 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 3 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Crusty / sliced bread300 g
  • Cheddar180 g
  • Milk80 ml
  • Dijon mustard15 g
  • Soy sauce5 ml
  • Malt vinegar5 ml
  • Butter20 g
  • Plain flour15 g
  • Tinned chopped tomatoes400 g
  • Onions100 g
  • Vegetable stock (from cubes or concentrate)300 ml prepared
  • Olive oil15 ml
  • Salad leaves150 g
  • Lemons0.5
  • Black pepper¼ tsp
  • Salt¼ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Chop onion and soften in 10 ml olive oil for 6 minutes. Add tomatoes, stock and salt; simmer for 15 minutes, then blend into a light soup.
  2. Melt butter in a small saucepan and stir in flour for 1 minute. Gradually whisk in milk to make a thick paste. Remove from the heat and stir in grated cheddar, mustard, soy, malt vinegar and pepper.
  3. Toast six slices of bread lightly on both sides. Spread with the cheese mixture and grill on a tray for 3–5 minutes until bubbling and browned; watch closely.
  4. Dress salad leaves with the remaining 5 ml olive oil and lemon juice. Serve with rarebit and small bowls of soup.
Kitchen note

Soy sauce and malt vinegar provide the savoury tang here, avoiding a special bottle of Worcestershire sauce.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
