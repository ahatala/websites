Prawn saganaki

Prawns in a rich tomato sauce, with feta, bread and Greek salad.

Serves 4Shared seafood meal35 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Raw peeled prawns600 g
  • Tinned chopped tomatoes800 g
  • Onions150 g
  • Garlic cloves3
  • Feta200 g
  • Olive oil40 ml
  • Dried oregano1 tsp
  • Fresh parsley15 g
  • Crusty / sliced bread400 g
  • Tomatoes300 g
  • Cucumber300 g
  • Red onions75 g
  • Lemons1
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Black pepper½ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Soften chopped onion in 25 ml oil in an ovenproof frying pan for 8 minutes. Add chopped garlic and oregano for 1 minute, then tinned tomatoes, salt and pepper. Simmer for 12–15 minutes until thickened.
  2. Pat the thawed, peeled prawns dry. Stir into the sauce, crumble 150 g feta over the top and bake for 8–12 minutes until the prawns are opaque throughout and cooked through.
  3. Chop fresh tomatoes, cucumber and red onion. Dress with the remaining 15 ml oil and half the lemon juice; top with the remaining 50 g feta.
  4. Sprinkle chopped parsley over the prawns. Serve with bread, salad and the remaining lemon cut into wedges.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
