Scallion pancakes

Flaky, savoury pancakes with a tangy dipping sauce.

Serves 44 pancakes · light lunch50 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Plain flour320 g
  • Spring onions100 g
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)55 ml
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Soy sauce30 ml
  • Rice vinegar20 ml
  • Toasted sesame oil5 ml

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Mix 300 g flour with salt. Carefully stir in 180 ml freshly boiled water. When cool enough to handle, knead for 5 minutes until smooth; cover and rest for 20 minutes. Reserve 20 g flour for rolling.
  2. Divide into four. Roll each into a thin rectangle, brush with some of 25 ml oil and scatter with chopped spring onions. Roll into a log, coil into a spiral and flatten gently. Rest for 5 minutes, then roll into 16–18 cm discs.
  3. Use the remaining 30 ml oil to pan-fry the pancakes over medium heat for 3–4 minutes per side, until golden and cooked inside. Press lightly with a spatula as they cook.
  4. Mix soy, vinegar, sesame oil and 20 ml water. Cut pancakes into wedges and serve with the dipping sauce.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
