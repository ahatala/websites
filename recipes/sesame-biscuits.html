Sunday / Dessert

Sesame biscuits & ice cream

A simple sesame-scented finale using everyday baking ingredients.

Serves 4Makes about 12 biscuits · replaces sesame balls30 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Plain flour120 g
  • Butter70 g
  • Caster sugar50 g
  • Sesame seeds40 g
  • Milk20 ml
  • Baking powder½ tsp
  • Vanilla ice cream300 g

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Heat the oven to 170°C fan. Beat softened butter and sugar until creamy. Mix flour with baking powder and 25 g sesame seeds, then stir into the butter with the milk to make a soft dough.
  2. Divide into twelve balls and flatten on a lined tray, leaving space between them. Press the remaining 15 g sesame seeds onto the tops.
  3. Bake for 14–18 minutes until the edges are pale gold. Leave on the tray for 5 minutes, then cool on a rack.
  4. Serve three biscuits each with vanilla ice cream.
Kitchen note

An easy family substitution for sesame balls, not a traditional version of that dish. Sesame remains an ingredient; only peanuts are excluded.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
