Monday / Dinner

Souvlaki night

Warm pitas, lemony chicken, golden halloumi and a table full of toppings.

Serves 43 chicken + 1 halloumi40 min + 20 min marinating
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Boneless chicken450 g
  • Halloumi150 g
  • Pita breads4 pieces
  • Greek yogurt250 g
  • Cucumber400 g
  • Tomatoes400 g
  • Red onions100 g
  • Lemons1
  • Garlic cloves2
  • Olive oil30 ml
  • Dried oregano2 tsp
  • Salt¾ tsp
  • Black pepper¼ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Cube the chicken. Mix olive oil, half the lemon juice, 1 crushed garlic clove, oregano, half the salt and the pepper. Reserve one quarter in a clean bowl for the halloumi; coat the chicken in the rest and refrigerate for 20 minutes.
  2. Grate 150 g cucumber, squeeze dry and mix with the yogurt, remaining garlic, remaining lemon juice and salt to make tzatziki. Slice the remaining cucumber, tomatoes and onion.
  3. Thread chicken onto skewers (soak wooden skewers first). Grill or pan-fry over medium-high heat for 10–14 minutes, turning, until the centre reaches 75°C for 30 seconds.
  4. Cut halloumi into thick pieces, coat in its reserved marinade and cook in a separate pan for 2–3 minutes per side. Warm the pitas and assemble with tzatziki and vegetables.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
