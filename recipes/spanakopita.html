Spanakopita & Greek salad

A crackly spinach-and-feta pie for the whole table.

Serves 4Meat-free for everyone65 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Frozen chopped spinach600 g
  • Feta300 g
  • Filo pastry250 g
  • Eggs2
  • Onions150 g
  • Fresh dill15 g
  • Olive oil60 ml
  • Tomatoes400 g
  • Cucumber300 g
  • Red onions75 g
  • Lemons0.5
  • Dried oregano1 tsp
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Black pepper½ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C fan. Thaw the spinach and squeeze it very dry. Chop the onion and soften in 15 ml oil for 8 minutes; let cool slightly.
  2. Mix spinach, onion, 200 g crumbled feta, eggs, chopped dill, pepper and half the salt. Brush a roughly 20 × 25 cm dish with a little of 30 ml oil. Layer half the filo in it, brushing lightly between sheets. Keep unused filo under a damp towel.
  3. Add the filling, cover with the remaining filo and brush with the rest of that 30 ml oil. Tuck in the edges, score the top into four portions and bake for 35–40 minutes until crisp and the filling is set.
  4. Chop tomatoes, cucumber and red onion. Dress with the remaining 15 ml oil, lemon juice, oregano and salt. Top with the remaining 100 g feta and serve with the pie.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
