Chicken & paneer tikka masala

One gently spiced sauce, two pans, rice and warm naan.

Serves 43 chicken + 1 paneer45 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Boneless chicken450 g
  • Paneer150 g
  • Greek yogurt100 g
  • Onions200 g
  • Garlic cloves3
  • Fresh ginger20 g
  • Tinned chopped tomatoes400 g
  • Double cream150 ml
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)30 ml
  • Garam masala2 tsp
  • Ground cumin1 tsp
  • Ground coriander1 tsp
  • Paprika1 tsp
  • Turmeric½ tsp
  • Salt¾ tsp
  • Basmati rice (dry)300 g
  • Naan breads4 pieces

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Cook the rice according to its packet. Dice the onion and soften in 15 ml oil for 8 minutes. Add grated ginger, crushed garlic and all the spices; stir for 1 minute.
  2. Add tomatoes and salt, simmer for 15 minutes, then stir in cream and yogurt over low heat. Divide this meat-free sauce between two pans, three quarters for chicken and one quarter for paneer.
  3. Cube the chicken. Brown in 10 ml oil for 5 minutes, add to its sauce and simmer for 12–15 minutes until cooked through, reaching 75°C for 30 seconds in the centre.
  4. Cube paneer and brown in the remaining 5 ml oil in a separate pan for 5 minutes. Add to its sauce and simmer for 5 minutes. Warm naan according to the packet and serve with rice.
Kitchen note

Paneer can be replaced gram-for-gram with firm tofu if it is missing from the cheese shelf.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
