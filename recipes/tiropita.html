Sunday / Lunch / snack

Little tiropita

Golden feta parcels; eight small pies for four.

Serves 4Meat-free for everyone40 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Filo pastry200 g
  • Feta200 g
  • Greek yogurt80 g
  • Eggs1
  • Butter40 g
  • Black pepper¼ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C fan. Mash feta with yogurt, egg and pepper. Melt the butter.
  2. Cut filo into strips about 10 cm wide. Use two layers for each of eight parcels, brushing lightly with butter. Keep the remaining pastry covered with a damp towel.
  3. Put one eighth of the filling at one end of each strip. Fold a corner over into a triangle, then continue folding to the end, enclosing the filling.
  4. Brush with remaining butter and bake on a lined tray for 20–25 minutes until golden and the filling is set. Cool for 5 minutes.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
