Friday / Dinner

Toad in the hole

Sausages in puffy batter, onion gravy, peas and carrots.

Serves 43 pork + 1 vegetarian sausage portion55 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 4 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Pork sausages450 g
  • Vegetarian sausages150 g
  • Plain flour160 g
  • Eggs3
  • Milk250 ml
  • Rapeseed oil (including frying oil)35 ml
  • Onions300 g
  • Vegetable stock (from cubes or concentrate)500 ml prepared
  • Butter20 g
  • Soy sauce10 ml
  • Dried thyme½ tsp
  • Carrots400 g
  • Frozen peas300 g
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Black pepper½ tsp

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Heat the oven to 210°C fan. Whisk 140 g flour, eggs, milk and salt into a smooth batter. Rest while the oven heats. Place pork sausages with 20 ml oil in a medium baking dish and vegetarian sausages with 10 ml oil in a small separate dish. Roast for 15 minutes.
  2. Pour three quarters of the batter around the pork sausages and one quarter around the vegetarian sausages. Return to the oven for 25–30 minutes without opening the door, until puffed and golden and the pork sausages reach 75°C for 30 seconds.
  3. For gravy, slice the onions and soften in butter and the remaining 5 ml oil for 15 minutes. Stir in the remaining 20 g flour, then gradually add stock, soy sauce, thyme and pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Peel and slice carrots; steam or boil for 8–10 minutes. Cook peas according to the packet. Serve with both versions of the toad and shared vegetarian gravy.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
