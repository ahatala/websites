Saturday / Dessert

Sticky toffee pudding

A small date sponge with warm toffee sauce and ice cream.

Serves 3Meat-free · small bake for 350 min
Weekly shopping list

What you’ll need

Total for all 3 portions, including sides and any separate version. Water is listed in the method.

  • Pitted dates90 g
  • Bicarbonate of soda½ tsp
  • Butter60 g
  • Brown sugar100 g
  • Eggs1
  • Plain flour90 g
  • Baking powder1 tsp
  • Vanilla extract½ tsp
  • Double cream100 ml
  • Vanilla ice cream180 g

Let’s cook.

Oven temperatures are for fan ovens. For conventional ovens, use about 20°C higher.

  1. Heat the oven to 170°C fan. Chop dates and pour over 120 ml boiling water. Stir in bicarbonate of soda and stand for 10 minutes; mash with a fork.
  2. Beat 35 g softened butter with 40 g brown sugar. Beat in the egg and vanilla, then fold in flour, baking powder and the date mixture.
  3. Line a small roughly 15 cm baking dish and add the batter. Bake for 25–30 minutes until a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean.
  4. For sauce, melt the remaining 25 g butter and 60 g sugar with the cream. Simmer for 3 minutes, stirring. Spoon over three portions of warm pudding and serve with ice cream.

Cooking times vary with equipment and ingredient size. Cooking guidance: where a core temperature is specified, check with a clean food thermometer.
